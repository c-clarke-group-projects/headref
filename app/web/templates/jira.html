What Headref tracks from Jira

  • Assigned Issues: In-progress, open, and review tickets assigned to each team member.
  • Sprint Blockers: Issues marked as blocked, flagged, or missing linked code for multiple days.
  • Jira & GitHub Conflicts: Cross-system discrepancies, such as Jira tickets marked "Done" while PRs remain open, or tickets marked "In Progress" with merged PRs.
  • Zero Member Verification: Issues and members are tracked automatically without manual account configuration.
